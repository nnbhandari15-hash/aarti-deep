# Privacy Policy

**App:** आरतीदीप (Android, package `com.nitinbhandari.aartideep`)
**Developer:** Nitin Bhandari
**Effective date:** 07/11/2026
**Contact:** nnb.developer@gmail.com

## Summary

आरतीदीप is an offline devotional app. It has no accounts and no servers. The developer does not collect, receive, share or sell any personal information.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not ask you to sign up or log in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

- the items you mark as favourites,
- the playlists you create, including their names and the order of items,
- your reading settings, such as text size and keep-screen-on.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage in Android settings. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this app data in your Google account backup. That is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by us.

## 3. Camera and QR codes

- **Creating a QR code:** A QR code made from a playlist contains only the playlist's name and the identifiers of the items in it. It does not contain personal information.
- **Scanning a QR code:** If you choose to scan, the camera, or a scanner provided by your device's system components, is used only to read the QR code. We do not store, upload or share camera images. Components provided by Google Play services are covered by Google's privacy policy.
- **Sharing a QR image:** If you share a QR image through Android's share menu, it goes to the app you choose, which has its own privacy policy.

## 4. Third-party services

The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. The app is distributed through Google Play, which processes information such as installs and updates under Google's own privacy policy.

## 5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## 6. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's protections, such as a screen lock and regular system updates.

## 7. Your choices

You can delete all of the app's data at any time by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question, contact us at the address above.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and the effective date before the change is released.

## 9. Contact

nnb.developer@gmail.com
